Dealing With Appliance Leak Water Damage in Hartford

Most of the appliance leaks we see trace back to three machines. A washing machine supply hose can split and run at full pressure until someone shuts it off. Dishwashers usually leak a little at a time, at the drain hose or door seal, hidden beneath the cabinets. The ice maker line on a refrigerator is a thin tube, and it can drip for months behind the unit. Cleanup is different for each of them.

Our Hartford crew starts by pulling the appliance out and finding where the water went. Kitchen leaks tend to end up beneath the cabinets and underneath the floor. In laundry rooms it means the wall behind the machine and the rooms next door, or the ceiling below if the laundry is upstairs. We extract what is standing, measure what has soaked in, and set drying equipment sized to the wet area.

Right Now

What to Do Right Now

  1. Shut the Machine DownStop the cycle and unplug the machine if the outlet and floor around it are dry. When either one is wet, turn the power off at the breaker.
  2. Shut Off the Water LineWashers have hot and cold valves on the wall behind them. The dishwasher valve is under the kitchen sink. A refrigerator line has a small valve below or behind the unit.
  3. Soak Up Standing WaterUse towels or a mop on whatever water is within reach, which keeps it from spreading beneath flooring and cabinets. Use a vacuum only if it is a wet and dry model.
  4. Keep the Door ClosedDo not open a washer or dishwasher that is still full. When the door opens mid-cycle, the full load of water can pour out on the floor.
  5. Document What FailedTake pictures of the hose, valve or fitting that failed and keep the part. Both the appliance maker and your insurer may want to look at it.
Coverage

Does Insurance Cover It?

When a machine overflows unexpectedly or a hose bursts, that is typically treated as sudden and accidental, which many homeowners policies cover. Payment typically goes toward the damage the water did, and the appliance or the part that failed is not included. A claim is harder when an ice maker line or dishwasher seeped slowly, since coverage usually does not extend to gradual leaks. How old the hose was and how it was maintained may also come up with some insurers. Hold on to the part that failed, photograph everything ahead of cleanup, and review what your policy says.

Pricing

What Affects the Cost

  • How long the leak ran before it was found, especially with a slow drip behind an appliance.
  • Whether mold has started and has to be removed before the area can be dried.
  • If it was gray discharge, which must be sanitized, or clean water from the supply.
  • How many rooms the water reached, including any ceiling below an upstairs laundry.

The Usual Culprits

What Causes It

  • Washer supply hoses made of rubber that get weak over the years and burst from the pressure.
  • A dishwasher with a failed pump seal, a cracked drain hose or a worn door gasket.
  • Installation mistakes like a loose supply line connection or a dishwasher that does not sit level.
  • Too much detergent or an overloaded drum that makes the machine overflow.
  • Refrigerators that leak at floor level because the drain pan has cracked or the defrost drain is clogged.
What Is at Risk

What Happens if You Wait

Adjoining Rooms

A washer hose can release water for hours. Hallways, bedrooms and closets get wet as it travels under the walls, and carpet and pad are soaked a good distance from the laundry.

Flooring and Subfloor

Hardwood, vinyl plank and laminate all let water slip underneath. The boards swell or cup, while the subfloor below remains wet well after the top looks dry.

Baseboards and Wall Bottoms

Water standing on a laundry or kitchen floor wicks up into baseboards and the bottom of the drywall. Often the worst of it is on the wall in back of the appliance, where it went unseen.

Downstairs Ceiling Damage

The ceiling under an upper floor laundry catches whatever water makes it past the floor above. The drywall stains and sags, and the insulation above it stays wet.
